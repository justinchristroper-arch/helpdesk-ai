# Laptop Damage Procedure
Demo / Synthetic IT Policy. Owner: Workplace IT. Version 1.0.

## Immediate action
Stop using a damaged company laptop. For liquid spills or visible electrical damage, disconnect power if safe and do not switch the device back on. Report the incident to IT support with the asset tag, description, and photos if safe to obtain. Do not attempt repairs yourself.

## Repair and replacement
IT assesses the device and arranges approved repair or replacement. Your manager approves a temporary loan device when business continuity requires it. Do not ship equipment or use a third-party repair shop without IT approval.
