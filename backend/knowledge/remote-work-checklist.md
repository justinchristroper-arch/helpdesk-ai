# Remote Work IT Checklist
Demo / Synthetic IT Policy. Owner: Workplace IT. Version 1.0.

## Before remote work
Use an approved managed laptop, install pending company updates, confirm MFA access, and test the VPN when internal services require it. Keep the service desk contact details accessible. Store work files only in approved company storage.

## While working remotely
Lock your screen when away, avoid shared devices, and keep confidential work out of public view. Use approved communication tools. Report lost equipment or suspicious account activity to IT support immediately.
