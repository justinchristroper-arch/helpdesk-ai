# MFA Reset Guide
Demo / Synthetic IT Policy. Owner: Identity Support. Version 1.0.

## MFA stops working
Check that your phone has network access and automatic time enabled. If your authenticator is unavailable or your phone is lost, contact IT support and request an MFA reset. Support verifies identity through the registered employee recovery process before resetting enrollment. Do not approve unexpected sign-in prompts.

## Re-enrollment
After identity verification, IT issues a time-limited enrollment link through the registered recovery channel. Register the new authenticator and complete a test sign-in. Never send support a password, recovery code, or one-time MFA code.
