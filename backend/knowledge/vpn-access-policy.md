# VPN Access Policy
Demo / Synthetic IT Policy. Owner: IT Operations. Version 1.0.

## Request VPN access
Submit an Access Request ticket in the demo IT service portal. Select VPN access, state the business reason, and attach your manager's approval. IT verifies your employment status and managed laptop before granting access. MFA enrollment is required before first connection.

## Connection problems
Confirm your internet connection, open the approved VPN client, and authenticate with your company account and MFA. If the connection fails, record the error message and contact IT support. Never share passwords or MFA codes.
