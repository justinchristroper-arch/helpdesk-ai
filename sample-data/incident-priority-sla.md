# IT Incident Priority and SLA Guide
Demo / Synthetic IT Policy. Owner: Service Desk. Version 1.0.

## High priority P2
A high-priority P2 incident significantly affects a department and has no reasonable workaround. The initial response target is 1 business hour. The restoration target is 8 business hours. These are separate targets, not guaranteed resolution times. Business hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 local company time, excluding company holidays.

## Critical priority P1
A critical P1 incident is a company-wide outage or confirmed active security incident. Contact the emergency IT support channel immediately. The initial response target is 15 minutes, monitored around the clock. This synthetic policy does not define a guaranteed resolution time.

## Normal priority P3
A normal-priority P3 incident affects an individual with a workable alternative. The initial response target is 1 business day. IT prioritizes restoration according to impact and available workarounds.
